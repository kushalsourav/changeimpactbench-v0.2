# V6 repair verification (additional versioned task skill)

Use the fixed budget in the *active task prompt*, not the historical V4/V5
skill's three-simulation limit. The V6 budget is five accepted simulations.
Before committing, inspect local prerequisites and evaluate enough candidates to
establish a unique cheapest **complete and safe** repair. Counterfactual tasks
may include quorum constraints and conditional repair operations; their effects
must be observed through tools. A simulation is read-only. A failed readiness
precondition yields no effect; do not assume the plan took effect. Commit only
one action in the sandbox and submit the initial impact inventory, hypothesis,
mechanistic explanation, evidence citations and rationale.
